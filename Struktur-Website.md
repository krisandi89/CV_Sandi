### 1️⃣ HERO SECTION (Above the Fold)

**Isi:**

- Profile image (center / top)
    
- Headline utama
    
    > “Hi, I’m [Name]”
    
- Sub-headline
    
    > “[Role / Position]”
    
- Short value statement (1–2 baris)
    
- Primary CTA:
    
    - `View My Work`
        
- Secondary CTA:
    
    - `Download CV` / `Contact`
        

**Catatan penting**

- Ini adalah **anchor branding**
    
- Jangan terlalu panjang
    
- Fokus pada _who you are + what value you bring_
    

---

### 2️⃣ SIDE / FLOATING NAVIGATION

**Isi icon-based navigation:**

- Home
    
- About / Skills
    
- Portfolio
    
- Education / Experience
    
- Languages / Certifications
    
- Contact
    

**Fungsi**

- One-page scroll navigation
    
- Sticky
    
- Visual identity kuat
    

---

### 3️⃣ SKILLS SECTION

**Layout**

- Card
    
- Skill icon di kiri
    
- Progress bar di kanan
    

**Isi**

- 3–6 skill utama saja
    
- Persentase → indikasi, bukan klaim absolut
    

**Tujuan**

- Kredibilitas teknis
    
- Cepat dipindai (scan-friendly)
    

---

### 4️⃣ PORTFOLIO SECTION

**Layout**

- Grid cards (2–4 kolom)
    
- Thumbnail preview
    
- Title
    
- Short description
    
- Optional tag (Web, UI, Dashboard, App)
    

**Best practice**

- Klik → modal / detail page
    
- Fokus ke **hasil**, bukan proses panjang
    

---

### 5️⃣ EDUCATION & CERTIFICATION

**Split layout**

- Kiri: Education
    
- Kanan: Certifications
    

**Isi ringkas**

- Nama institusi
    
- Program / sertifikasi
    
- Tahun
    

**Tujuan**

- Validasi profesional
    
- Trust signal
    

---

### 6️⃣ LANGUAGES / ADDITIONAL SKILLS

**Layout**

- Progress bar horizontal
    
- Label bahasa / skill
    
- Level (% atau label)
    

**Catatan**

- Ini opsional, tapi menambah kesan global & profesional
    

---

### 7️⃣ FOOTER / CLOSING STATEMENT

**Isi**

- Short personal statement
    
- “Always learning and improving”
    
- Social icons (LinkedIn, GitHub, IG, Email)
    

---

## 3. BRAND POSITIONING (Ringkas)

Jika diringkas, brand image website ini adalah:

> **“Digital professional with strong technical credibility, modern aesthetic, and calm confidence.”**
